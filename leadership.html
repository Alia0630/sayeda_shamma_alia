Leadership & collaboration

Organizing international
research challenges

Between 2019 and 2021, I organized and co-organized five international activity recognition challenges at premier venues including UbiComp. Each challenge brought together researchers from multiple countries and resulted in a peer-reviewed publication.

🌍

Cross-cultural leadership

Coordinated teams of researchers from Japan, France, and beyond — managing communication across languages, time zones, and academic cultures.

📊

Dataset curation

Prepared, validated, and distributed real-world sensor datasets to participating teams worldwide, ensuring data quality under tight timelines.

📝

Publishing results

Each challenge produced a peer-reviewed publication summarizing methodology, participating teams, and comparative results.
