About me

Researcher, analyst,
and lifelong learner

I am Sayeda Shamma Alia, a 4th-year PhD candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Concordia University, Montreal.

My research sits at the intersection of machine learning, activity recognition, and assistive technology for elderly populations.

Before my PhD, I completed my MEng in Human Intelligence Systems at Kyushu Institute of Technology in Japan, where I was part of SozoLab — a pioneering lab in sensor-based human activity recognition. I organized and participated in five international research challenges, building deep experience leading diverse global teams.

I have lived and worked across Bangladesh, Japan, and Canada — each country has shaped how I think about people, patterns, and the quiet rhythms of daily life that my research tries to understand.
