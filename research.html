Research

Understanding daily life
from the inside out

As populations age globally, understanding daily routines becomes critical for early detection of cognitive and physical decline. My PhD work uses machine learning and sensor data to make aging technology smarter and more humane.

Why it matters

The bigger picture

By 2050, the global population aged 60 and over will double. Detecting subtle changes in daily routines — a slightly later wake time, a skipped meal, a shorter walk — can serve as early indicators of cognitive or functional decline, enabling earlier intervention and better quality of life.

My research contributes to the field of ambient assisted living (AAL) — building systems that support independent living for older adults unobtrusively, using the sensor data already available in smart homes and wearable devices.
